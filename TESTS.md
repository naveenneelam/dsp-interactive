## 5. Known testing gaps

- **Not tested with a screen reader** (NVDA/JAWS/VoiceOver). ARIA labels, landmarks, focus styles, and keyboard paths are present, but a formal assistive-technology audit is future work.
- **Chromium only.** Playwright's Firefox/WebKit engines were not run. The app uses standard ES modules, Canvas 2D, ResizeObserver, and localStorage.
- **Audio is not auditioned.** The audio lab is visual/quantitative; no sound is played.
- **No load/stress testing.** Labs are single-user, local, and lightweight; very large screens or low-end GPUs were not benchmarked.
- **Numerical tests are not exhaustive.** They check representative cases and invariants, not every possible parameter combination or every lab's control state.
- **Per-concept quiz diagnostics use a keyword heuristic;** no psychometric validation was performed.
