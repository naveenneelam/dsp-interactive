How to learn here

Every chapter has four layers: Concepts (read), Lab (manipulate), Worked example (reason), and Practice (check). Mark sections understood to build your progress. Quiz results feed the review queue.

The Source coverage page maps every section of the book to this course, including the PDF page number.

Source policy. Explanations are authored summaries grounded in Smith's text and page-referenced. Modern examples and some engineering guidance are supplemental; they are not presented as claims of the source. Where the source is ambiguous, the coverage page records it.
